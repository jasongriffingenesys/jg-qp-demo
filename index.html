Finding it hard to pay your bills?

You're not alone, and you don't have to sort it out on your own. Our Consumer Support team can talk things through with you and point you to help, including charity fuel vouchers and your council's hardship support.

  • Energy: ask your supplier about emergency credit and their Priority Services Register.
  • Council tax and water: contact them early about payment plans and discounts.
  • Free debt advice: StepChange, National Debtline, Citizens Advice and MoneyHelper.
